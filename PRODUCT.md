# PRODUCT.md

## 1. The job
When I don't know what to do for the day and start procrastinating, I want to know which income task to do first, so I can finish at least one task that moves me toward my goal instead of drifting.
Who: a freelancer chasing a goal who wakes up with no clear plan.
Today they use: their head, Notes, ChatGPT, scrolling.

## 2. The switch
Push: no income work done, guilt and stress.
Pull: seeing work done, feeling proud.
Anxiety: "It'll make me feel guilty when I miss tasks." Answer: missed tasks move to tomorrow kindly, no red marks.
Habit: head, Notes, ChatGPT. Answer: 3 income tasks ready each morning, plus a progress count.

## 3. The core flow
1. Morning: "what should I do first?"
2. Open the app.
3. See 3 tasks in order, missed ones included, no red marks.
4. Do task 1, tap done.
5. See progress.
Job done: at least one income task finished.
Steps today: 7. With my product: 3.
Must not happen: red marks, "you failed", a list of 10, vague tasks like "work on outreach".

## 4. Onboarding
1. They open the link and see "3 tasks a day. Miss one? It moves to tomorrow, no guilt." (removes the guilt worry)
2. They describe their goal in their own words.
3. The AI suggests a task list and asks "Should this be your checklist?" They tap Yes or edit it.
4. They see today's checklist.
Login only after step 4. No name, profile or tour.

## 5. v1
Does: goal in, AI checklist, today's 3 tasks, tap done, missed tasks move without guilt. Goal asked once and saved; opens straight to tasks after that.
Doesn't: entrepreneurs, reminders, calendar, streaks, sharing, more than one goal.
How I'll know: someone opens it again the next morning without being told.

## 6. The riskiest guess
Freelancers will come back the next morning, because getting 3 tasks without typing is easier than asking ChatGPT.
Check: act as the app on WhatsApp for 2 friends for 2 days.
What happened: not done yet.

## 7. Milestones
1. I can type my goal and the AI gives me 3 specific income tasks that I can understand and start immediately without deciding what to do next.
2. I can see today's 3 tasks and tap them done.
3. I can miss a task and see it move to tomorrow, with no guilt message.
4. I can see how many tasks I've done toward my goal.
Last: I can close it, reopen it, and my goal and tasks are still there.
